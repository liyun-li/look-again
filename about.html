LOOK AGAIN

Each round is the photo from a real, current FBI wanted poster, and nothing else. Guess what the person is wanted for, in one tap: 3 points on gut alone, or peek at one written clue first and score 1. Then the real charge, the FBI page and the full poster appear. The end screen shows your gut versus reality: how often you misjudged, and the face that surprised you most. The joke is on our assumptions, never on the people in the photos.

Play today’s 5 faces (the same for everyone, new at midnight Taiwan time, with a streak) or endless mode (3 strikes). Only wanted fugitives and suspects are guessed. Missing people, kidnapping victims and “seeking information” posters are never scored or guessed: after each game a few missing people appear in a separate “Help find them” strip, with a tip link.

Only clear photos are used: FBI seal placeholders, silhouettes, sketches and hard-to-see portraits are skipped.

Your casebook stays on your device. When a poster you played is taken off the FBI list, it shows as “No longer on the FBI list”. The FBI removes posters for many reasons, so the game doesn’t guess why; it only says captured, located and so on when the FBI’s own data says so.

Seen someone? Submit a tip at tips.fbi.gov ↗

Please keep in mind

  • Charges are allegations. Everyone is presumed innocent until proven guilty.
  • Charges and case text are the FBI’s own wording (the short Chinese summary of a charge is a translation of its key words). Written descriptions of race, ethnicity and appearance are left out; the official FBI page has the full poster.
  • Left out entirely: child-exploitation cases, unidentified remains, homicide and sexual-assault files, cases about a death, and anything graphic.
  • If you have urgent information, call 911 or 1-800-CALL-FBI.

Data

Source: FBI Wanted, via the public FBI Wanted API. Works of the U.S. federal government are in the public domain. This game is not affiliated with or endorsed by the FBI.

Your browser never contacts FBI servers. Once a day a scheduled job reads the API slowly, saves small copies of new portraits, removes cases the FBI has taken down (for example, people found or arrested), and publishes the result here.

再看一眼

每一題只給你一張真實、仍在公告中的 FBI 通緝照。點一下，猜這個人為什麼被通緝：憑直覺猜中得 3 分；先偷看一條文字線索再猜中得 1 分。接著揭曉真正的罪名、FBI 頁面和完整公告。結算畫面會告訴你「直覺 vs 現實」：看走眼幾次、哪張臉最出乎意料。笑點是我們自己的刻板印象，從來不是照片裡的人。

可以玩今日 5 張臉（每個人都一樣，台灣時間午夜更新，還能累積連續天數），或無盡模式（三振出局）。只有在逃的通緝犯與嫌疑人會拿來猜。失蹤者、綁架受害者與「徵求線索」公告不計分、也不拿來猜：每局結束後會另外放幾位失蹤者在「幫忙找人」區塊，附上提供線索的連結。

只使用清楚的照片：FBI 徽章佔位圖、剪影、素描和看不清楚的照片都會略過。

辦案筆記只存在你的裝置上。你玩過的公告若被 FBI 撤下，會顯示「已不在 FBI 名單上」。FBI 撤下公告的原因很多，遊戲不會替它下結論；只有 FBI 自己的資料標示已逮捕、已尋獲等狀態時，才會這樣寫。

見過公告上的人？到 tips.fbi.gov 提供線索 ↗

請留意

  • 起訴內容僅為指控，未經法院定罪前，任何人皆推定無罪。
  • 罪名與案情為 FBI 英文原文（中文的罪名重點是關鍵字翻譯）；遊戲中略去種族、族裔與外貌的文字描述，完整公告請見 FBI 官方頁面。
  • 完全排除：兒童剝削案件、身分不明遺體、凶殺與性侵檔案、與死亡相關的案件，以及任何血腥內容。
  • 若有緊急資訊，請撥打 911 或 1-800-CALL-FBI。

資料來源

來源：FBI Wanted，透過公開的 FBI Wanted API 取得。美國聯邦政府作品屬公有領域。本遊戲與 FBI 無關，亦未獲其背書。

你的瀏覽器不會連到 FBI 伺服器。系統每天排程一次，慢慢讀取 API、只為新案件存下縮小的照片、移除 FBI 已下架的案件（例如已尋獲或已逮捕），再發布到這裡。
