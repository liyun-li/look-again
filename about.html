LOOK AGAIN

Each round is the photo from a real, current U.S. federal wanted poster (FBI, DEA, Secret Service or U.S. Marshals), and nothing else. Guess what the person is wanted for, in one tap: 3 points on gut alone, or peek at one written clue first and score 1. Then the real charge, the agency that published it, its page and the full poster appear. The end screen shows your gut versus reality: how often you misjudged, and the face that surprised you most. The joke is on our assumptions, never on the people in the photos.

Play today’s 5 faces (the same for everyone, new at midnight Taiwan time, with a streak) or endless mode (3 strikes). Only wanted fugitives and suspects are guessed. Missing people, kidnapping victims and “seeking information” posters are never scored or guessed: after each game a few missing people appear in a separate “Help find them” strip, with a tip link.

Only clear photos are used: seal placeholders, silhouettes, sketches and hard-to-see portraits are skipped.

The categories follow the FBI’s own lists: Violent Crime, Criminal Enterprise, White-Collar Crime, Cyber’s Most Wanted, Counterintelligence and Terrorism, plus Drug trafficking for the DEA’s fugitives. Posters from other agencies are filed by the charge the agency itself lists. A case can have more than one right answer (every category its own list or charge names counts); every other option shown is one the case clearly doesn’t fit, so some rounds have only two or three choices. The poster’s agency is shown only after you guess, because it would give the answer away.

Your casebook stays on your device. When a poster you played is taken off its agency’s list, it shows as “No longer on the wanted list”. Agencies remove posters for many reasons, so the game doesn’t guess why; it only says captured, located and so on when the FBI’s own data says so. A few posters the FBI marks as captured stay in play for a while, marked “Captured”.

Seen someone? Submit a tip at tips.fbi.gov ↗

Please keep in mind

  • Charges are allegations. Everyone is presumed innocent until proven guilty.
  • Charges and case text are the agency’s own wording (the short Chinese summary of a charge is a translation of its key words). Written descriptions of race, ethnicity and appearance are left out; the agency’s official page has the full poster. Cases involving crimes against children are never used.
  • Left out entirely: child-exploitation cases, unidentified remains, homicide and sexual-assault files, cases about a death, and anything graphic.
  • If you have urgent information, call 911, or contact the agency named on the poster (FBI: 1-800-CALL-FBI).

Data

Source: FBI Wanted, via the public FBI Wanted API. Also the DEA fugitives list, the U.S. Secret Service Most Wanted and the U.S. Marshals 15 Most Wanted. Works of the U.S. federal government are in the public domain; agency seals and logos are not used. This game is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of these agencies.

Your browser never contacts any agency’s servers. Once a day a scheduled job reads the FBI API slowly (the other agencies’ lists once a week), saves small copies of new portraits, removes cases the agencies have taken down (for example, people found or arrested), and publishes the result here.

We count anonymous play stats (like rounds played and share taps), with no cookies and no personal data.

再看一眼

每一題只給你一張真實、仍在公告中的美國聯邦通緝照（FBI、DEA、特勤局或法警局）。點一下，猜這個人為什麼被通緝：憑直覺猜中得 3 分；先偷看一條文字線索再猜中得 1 分。接著揭曉真正的罪名、發布機關、官方頁面和完整公告。結算畫面會告訴你「直覺 vs 現實」：看走眼幾次、哪張臉最出乎意料。笑點是我們自己的刻板印象，從來不是照片裡的人。

可以玩今日 5 張臉（每個人都一樣，台灣時間午夜更新，還能累積連續天數），或無盡模式（三振出局）。只有在逃的通緝犯與嫌疑人會拿來猜。失蹤者、綁架受害者與「徵求線索」公告不計分、也不拿來猜：每局結束後會另外放幾位失蹤者在「幫忙找人」區塊，附上提供線索的連結。

只使用清楚的照片：徽章佔位圖、剪影、素描和看不清楚的照片都會略過。

分類照 FBI 自己的名單：暴力犯罪、犯罪集團、白領犯罪、網路犯罪頭號通緝（Cyber’s Most Wanted）、反情報、恐怖主義，另外 DEA 的通緝犯歸在「販毒」。其他機關的公告，依該機關自己列出的罪名歸類。一個案子可以有不只一個正確答案（它的名單或罪名提到的類別都算對）；題目裡其他選項一定是明顯不符的，所以有些題目只有兩、三個選項。發布機關會在你作答後才顯示，因為它會洩漏答案。

辦案筆記只存在你的裝置上。你玩過的公告若被機關撤下，會顯示「已不在通緝名單上」。機關撤下公告的原因很多，遊戲不會替它下結論；只有 FBI 自己的資料標示已逮捕、已尋獲等狀態時，才會這樣寫。少數 FBI 標示已落網的公告會暫時留在遊戲中，並標明「已落網」。

請留意

  • 起訴內容僅為指控，未經法院定罪前，任何人皆推定無罪。
  • 罪名與案情為該機關英文原文（中文的罪名重點是關鍵字翻譯）；遊戲中略去種族、族裔與外貌的文字描述，完整公告請見該機關官方頁面。涉及兒童的犯罪案件一律不使用。
  • 完全排除：兒童剝削案件、身分不明遺體、凶殺與性侵檔案、與死亡相關的案件，以及任何血腥內容。
  • 若有緊急資訊，請撥打 911，或聯絡公告上的機關（FBI：1-800-CALL-FBI）。

資料來源

來源：FBI Wanted，透過公開的 FBI Wanted API 取得。另外也使用 DEA 通緝犯名單、美國特勤局頭號通緝與美國法警局 15 大通緝要犯。美國聯邦政府作品屬公有領域；不使用任何機關的徽章或標誌。本遊戲與上述機關皆無關，亦未獲其背書。

你的瀏覽器不會連到任何機關的伺服器。系統每天排程一次，慢慢讀取 FBI API（其他機關名單每週一次）、只為新案件存下縮小的照片、移除機關已下架的案件（例如已尋獲或已逮捕），再發布到這裡。

只統計匿名遊玩數據（例如玩了幾題、按了幾次分享），不用 cookie，也不收集個人資料。
